iPhone 扫码，Mac 上键入

LibraScan 是一个不联网的二维码 / 条码扫描器。打开 Mac 上的 LibraScan，iPhone 扫到什么，就实时键入 Mac 当前聚焦的输入框——Excel、网页表单、终端都行，像一把无线扫码枪。

App Store 即将上架 下载 Mac 版 v1.0

iPhone 需要 iOS 18 或更新；Mac 需要 macOS 15 或更新。Mac 版经 Developer ID 签名并由 Apple 公证。

三步

  1. Mac 上打开 LibraScan

    它住在菜单栏。首次使用按引导开启「辅助功能」权限——macOS 把模拟键入归在这项权限下，LibraScan 只用它发送按键。

  2. iPhone 连上 Mac

    扫一扫页右上角点「键入到 Mac」，在附近 Mac 列表里选中你的那台，Mac 弹窗点「允许」。之后可自动重连，不必再确认。

  3. 扫码

    把光标放进任何输入框。iPhone 扫到的内容立刻出现在光标处，可选自动补回车或 Tab，连续扫就是连续录入。

做得少，但做对

扫描

  • QR、Data Matrix、Aztec、PDF417；EAN-13/8、UPC-E、Code 39/93/128、ITF-14、Codabar
  • 记录只存在 iPhone 本地，可搜索、可左滑删除
  • 一键导出 TXT，走系统分享
  • 支持捏合变焦与手电筒

键入桥

  • 中文、emoji 照样键入，与键盘布局无关
  • 断线时先排队，重连后按序补发（可关）
  • 每条都有回执：已键入 / 未授权 / 已暂停
  • 菜单栏一键急停；可配置结尾按键与键入间隔

隐私

  • 没有账号、没有服务器、没有统计
  • 相机画面不保存、不上传
  • Mac 端最近键入只在内存里保留 5 条
  • 信任列表随时可改、可清空

常见问题

Mac 版为什么不在 Mac App Store？

模拟键入依赖的系统权限本身与沙箱兼容，但这类应用的上架审核标准并不稳定。我们选择 Developer ID 签名加 Apple 公证的方式直接分发，安装体验与商店一致，不会被 Gatekeeper 拦下。

为什么需要「辅助功能」权限？

macOS 把「向其它应用发送键盘事件」归在辅助功能权限之下。LibraScan 只用它发送按键，不读取屏幕、不监听你的键盘。

我的数据会离开设备吗？

不会。扫描记录保存在 iPhone 本地；iPhone 到 Mac 的传输是加密的点对点连接，中间没有服务器，我们也看不到任何内容。

为什么密码框里打不进去？

macOS 的安全输入模式会屏蔽一切合成键盘事件，USB 扫码枪也一样。这是系统行为，不是故障。

连不上 Mac？

依次检查：Mac 上的 LibraScan 是否在运行、两台设备的 Wi-Fi 和蓝牙是否都开着、iPhone 设置里是否允许 LibraScan 使用本地网络。更多见支持页。

Scan on iPhone, type on Mac

LibraScan is a QR and barcode scanner that never touches the network. Run LibraScan on your Mac, and whatever your iPhone scans is typed into the focused text field — a spreadsheet, a web form, a terminal — like a wireless barcode scanner.

App Store coming soon Download for Mac v1.0

Requires iOS 18 or later on iPhone and macOS 15 or later on Mac. The Mac app is Developer ID–signed and notarized by Apple.

Three steps

  1. Open LibraScan on your Mac

    It lives in the menu bar. On first run it walks you through granting Accessibility permission — that's where macOS files "posting keyboard events", and it's all LibraScan uses it for.

  2. Connect your iPhone

    Tap “Type to Mac” on the scan screen, pick your Mac from the list, and click “Allow” on the Mac. After that it reconnects on its own.

  3. Scan

    Put the cursor in any text field. Whatever the iPhone scans shows up at the cursor, optionally followed by Return or Tab. Keep scanning, keep entering.

Little, done right

Scanning

  • QR, Data Matrix, Aztec, PDF417; EAN-13/8, UPC-E, Code 39/93/128, ITF-14, Codabar
  • History stays on the iPhone; searchable, swipe to delete
  • Export everything as a TXT via the share sheet
  • Pinch to zoom, torch for dark shelves

The bridge

  • Types CJK and emoji correctly, independent of keyboard layout
  • Queues scans while offline, replays them in order on reconnect (optional)
  • Every scan gets a receipt: typed, not authorized, paused
  • One-click pause in the menu bar; configurable suffix key and keystroke interval

Privacy

  • No account, no server, no analytics
  • Camera frames are never stored or uploaded
  • The Mac keeps only the last five items, in memory
  • Trusted devices can be removed at any time

Questions

Why isn't the Mac app on the Mac App Store?

The permission that synthetic typing relies on is compatible with the App Sandbox, but review outcomes for this kind of app are inconsistent. We distribute directly with Developer ID signing and Apple notarization instead — it installs like any other app and Gatekeeper is happy.

Why does it need Accessibility permission?

macOS files “send keyboard events to other apps” under Accessibility. LibraScan uses it to post keystrokes and nothing else — it doesn't read the screen or watch your keyboard.

Does my data leave my devices?

No. Scan history lives on the iPhone. The iPhone-to-Mac link is an encrypted peer-to-peer connection with no server in between, so there's nothing for us to see.

Why won't it type into a password field?

macOS Secure Input blocks all synthetic keyboard events — USB barcode scanners hit the same wall. That's the system working as designed.

Can't find the Mac?

Check, in order: LibraScan is running on the Mac; Wi-Fi and Bluetooth are on for both devices; LibraScan is allowed to use the Local Network in iPhone Settings. More on the support page.
