iPhone 扫码，Mac 上键入

LibraScan 是一个不联网的二维码 / 条码扫描器。打开 Mac 上的 LibraScan，iPhone 扫到什么，就实时键入 Mac 当前聚焦的输入框——Excel、网页表单、终端都行，像一把无线扫码枪。

即将登陆 App Store 下载 macOS 端键盘

iPhone 需要 iOS 18 或更新；Mac 需要 macOS 15 或更新。

三步

  1. Mac 上打开 LibraScan

    它住在菜单栏。首次使用按引导开启「辅助功能」权限——macOS 把模拟键入归在这项权限下，LibraScan 只用它发送按键。

  2. iPhone 连上 Mac

    扫一扫页右上角点「键入到 Mac」，在附近 Mac 列表里选中你的那台，Mac 弹窗点「允许」。之后可自动重连，不必再确认。

  3. 扫码

    把光标放进任何输入框。iPhone 扫到的内容立刻出现在光标处，可选自动补回车或 Tab，连续扫就是连续录入。

常见问题

为什么需要「辅助功能」权限？

macOS 把「向其它应用发送键盘事件」归在辅助功能权限之下。LibraScan 只用它发送按键，不读取屏幕、不监听你的键盘。

我的数据会离开设备吗？

不会。扫描记录保存在 iPhone 本地；iPhone 到 Mac 的传输是加密的点对点连接，中间没有服务器，我们也看不到任何内容。

找不到 Mac？
  • Mac 上的 LibraScan 要在运行（菜单栏有键盘图标），且没有正连着另一台 iPhone——它一次只服务一台。
  • 两台设备都要开 Wi-Fi 和蓝牙。不要求在同一路由器下，但都关着不行。
  • iPhone：设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 本地网络，确认 LibraScan 已允许。第一次拒绝后系统不会再问，只能在这里改。Mac 上同样检查一遍。
  • 公司网络有时会隔离设备之间的通信；换手机热点，或者把 Mac 的 Wi-Fi 关了再开走点对点，通常能绕过。
连上了，但 Mac 不键入？
  • 横幅显示「Mac 未授权」：回到 Mac 的辅助功能设置确认 LibraScan 已勾选；升级或移动过应用后，偶尔要先移除再重新添加。
  • 横幅显示「已暂停」：Mac 菜单栏里的「暂停键入」开着，关掉即可。
  • 打进去了但乱序或漏字：在 Mac 设置里把「键入间隔」调大（默认 3 ms，慢一些的应用需要 10 ms 以上）。
为什么密码框里打不进去？

macOS 的安全输入模式会屏蔽一切合成键盘事件——终端的安全输入、部分远程桌面也一样，USB 扫码枪同样打不进去。这是系统行为，不是故障。

iPhone 锁屏后怎么断开了？

锁屏或退到后台会主动断开，回到前台后自动重连上次的 Mac。断线期间扫到的码会排队（最多 50 条），重连后按顺序补发；不想补发可以在「键入到 Mac」面板里关掉。想换一台 Mac，先在面板里点「断开连接」。

记录能导出吗？

能。记录页左上角的导出按钮会生成一个 TXT 文件（每条含时间、码制与内容），通过系统分享发到你想去的地方。记录页支持搜索和左滑删除；垃圾桶会清空全部，不可撤销。

Scan on iPhone, type on Mac

LibraScan is a QR and barcode scanner that never touches the network. Run LibraScan on your Mac, and whatever your iPhone scans is typed into the focused text field — a spreadsheet, a web form, a terminal — like a wireless barcode scanner.

Coming soon to the App Store Download the Mac companion

Requires iOS 18 or later on iPhone and macOS 15 or later on Mac.

Three steps

  1. Open LibraScan on your Mac

    It lives in the menu bar. On first run it walks you through granting Accessibility permission — that's where macOS files "posting keyboard events", and it's all LibraScan uses it for.

  2. Connect your iPhone

    Tap “Type to Mac” on the scan screen, pick your Mac from the list, and click “Allow” on the Mac. After that it reconnects on its own.

  3. Scan

    Put the cursor in any text field. Whatever the iPhone scans shows up at the cursor, optionally followed by Return or Tab. Keep scanning, keep entering.

Questions

Why does it need Accessibility permission?

macOS files “send keyboard events to other apps” under Accessibility. LibraScan uses it to post keystrokes and nothing else — it doesn't read the screen or watch your keyboard.

Does my data leave my devices?

No. Scan history lives on the iPhone. The iPhone-to-Mac link is an encrypted peer-to-peer connection with no server in between, so there's nothing for us to see.

Can't find the Mac?
  • LibraScan must be running on the Mac (keyboard icon in the menu bar) and not already connected to another iPhone — it serves one at a time.
  • Both devices need Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on. The same router isn't required, but both radios off won't work.
  • iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network — make sure LibraScan is allowed. After a first refusal iOS won't ask again; this is the only place to change it. Check the same on the Mac.
  • Some office networks isolate devices from each other. A phone hotspot, or toggling the Mac's Wi-Fi off and on to fall back to peer-to-peer, usually gets around it.
Connected, but nothing is typed?
  • The banner says “Mac not authorized to type”: confirm LibraScan is checked under Accessibility on the Mac. After updating or moving the app it occasionally needs to be removed and re-added.
  • The banner says “Typing paused on Mac”: the “Pause typing” switch in the Mac menu is on. Turn it off.
  • Characters arrive out of order or go missing: raise “Keystroke interval” in the Mac settings (3 ms by default; slower apps want 10 ms or more).
Why won't it type into a password field?

macOS Secure Input blocks all synthetic keyboard events — the same goes for terminals in Secure Input mode, some remote-desktop sessions, and USB barcode scanners. That's the system working as designed.

Why did it disconnect when I locked the iPhone?

Locking the iPhone or sending the app to the background disconnects on purpose; it reconnects to the last Mac when you come back. Codes scanned meanwhile are queued (up to 50) and replayed in order — turn that off in the “Type to Mac” panel if you'd rather they weren't. To switch Macs, tap “Disconnect” in the panel first.

Can I export the history?

Yes. The export button at the top left of History produces one TXT file (time, symbology and content per record) and hands it to the share sheet. History supports search and swipe-to-delete; the trash button clears everything and can't be undone.
