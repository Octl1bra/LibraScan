隐私政策

适用于 LibraScan（iOS）与 LibraScan for Mac · 生效日期 2026-08-22

一句话版本：LibraScan 不收集、不存储、不传输任何关于你的数据。没有账号、没有服务器、没有第三方 SDK、没有统计分析、没有广告。下面是展开说明。

相机

iPhone 版使用相机识别二维码与条码。画面只在设备上实时处理，不会被保存、截图或上传。

扫描记录

扫到的内容与时间保存在 iPhone 本地数据库中，只有你能看到。你可以随时删除单条记录或清空全部。只有当你主动点「导出」时，记录才会以 TXT 文件的形式交给你选择的系统分享目标（隔空投送、备忘录、邮件等）——去向由你决定，LibraScan 不参与。

本地网络与 Mac 键入桥

当你使用「键入到 Mac」时，iPhone 通过 Apple 的 MultipeerConnectivity 框架与 Mac 建立点对点连接（点对点 Wi-Fi 或局域网），传输强制加密。扫到的内容只会发送到你在 Mac 上明确「允许」过的那台设备，中间没有任何服务器，我们无法看到内容。

Mac 端把最近 5 条键入内容保留在内存中供你查看或复制，退出应用即消失；信任过的 iPhone 名称与配对日期保存在 Mac 本地偏好设置中，可在设置里删除。

更新检查（仅 Mac 版）

Mac 版每天最多一次向 GitHub 的公开接口（api.github.com）查询本项目最新的发布版本，用来判断是否有新版。这是一次普通的 GET 请求，只带一个标明「LibraScan 及其版本号」的 User-Agent：不发送设备标识、系统版本、使用情况或任何其它信息。检查结果只在本机显示，发现新版本时也只是给出下载链接，不会自动下载或安装。GitHub 会像对待任何一次网站访问一样记录 IP 与时间，其隐私政策由 GitHub 自行提供。

可以在「设置 → 关于 → 自动检查更新」中关闭；关闭后除非你手动点「立即检查」，Mac 版不会发起任何网络请求。iPhone 版没有这项功能。

Mac 上的辅助功能权限

macOS 将「向其它应用发送键盘事件」归在辅助功能权限之下。LibraScan for Mac 只用这项权限发送按键，不读取屏幕内容，不监听键盘，不访问其它应用的界面。

第三方

两端应用均不包含任何第三方 SDK，不接入分析、崩溃上报或广告服务，不与任何第三方共享数据——因为根本没有数据可共享。

儿童

LibraScan 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不收集任何年龄段用户的个人信息。

变更

如本政策有变化，会更新本页面与生效日期。

Privacy Policy

Applies to LibraScan for iOS and LibraScan for Mac · Effective 2026-08-22

The short version: LibraScan does not collect, store, or transmit any data about you. There is no account, no server, no third-party SDK, no analytics, and no advertising. The details follow.

Camera

The iPhone app uses the camera to detect QR codes and barcodes. Frames are processed on the device in real time and are never saved, captured, or uploaded.

Scan history

Scanned content and timestamps are stored in a local database on your iPhone, visible only to you. You can delete individual records or clear everything at any time. Only when you tap “Export” is the history written to a TXT file and handed to the system share destination you choose (AirDrop, Notes, Mail, and so on) — where it goes is up to you; LibraScan is not involved.

Local network and the Mac bridge

When you use “Type to Mac”, the iPhone connects to your Mac peer-to-peer through Apple's MultipeerConnectivity framework (peer-to-peer Wi-Fi or your local network) with encryption required. Scanned content is sent only to the Mac you explicitly allowed, with no server in between, so we cannot see it.

The Mac app keeps the last five typed items in memory so you can review or copy them; they are gone when the app quits. Names and pairing dates of trusted iPhones are stored in the Mac's local preferences and can be removed in Settings.

Update checks (Mac app only)

At most once a day, the Mac app asks GitHub's public API (api.github.com) for this project's latest release to see whether a newer version exists. It is an ordinary GET carrying only a User-Agent that names LibraScan and its version: no device identifier, OS version, usage data, or anything else is sent. The result is shown only on your Mac, and a newer version is offered as a download link — nothing is downloaded or installed for you. GitHub logs the IP and timestamp as it would for any visit, under its own privacy policy.

You can turn it off under Settings → About → “Check for updates automatically”. With it off, the Mac app makes no network requests at all unless you press “Check Now”. The iPhone app has no such feature.

Accessibility permission on the Mac

macOS files “sending keyboard events to other apps” under the Accessibility permission. LibraScan for Mac uses it to post keystrokes and nothing else — it does not read the screen, monitor your keyboard, or access other apps' interfaces.

Third parties

Neither app contains third-party SDKs, analytics, crash reporting, or advertising, and no data is shared with anyone — there is no data to share.

Children

LibraScan is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from users of any age.

If this policy changes, this page and its effective date will be updated.
